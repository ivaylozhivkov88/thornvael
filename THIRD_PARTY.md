# Third-party material

Nothing from third parties is shipped as an asset yet. All icons and graphics are generated by scripts in `tools/` and are the project's own work.

Software dependencies (build-time only, not part of the shipped assets): Vite, TypeScript, vite-plugin-pwa and Workbox, Vitest. All are MIT or Apache-2.0 licensed.

Every future asset must appear in `assets.json` with license `own`, `CC0` or `OFL-1.1`, and any non-own asset must also be listed here with its author and source. CI (`npm run check:repo`) enforces this.
