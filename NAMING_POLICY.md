# Naming and text policy

- The game is called Thornvael. Names of other games, studios and their characters, places, items and spells are never used in the code, text, file names or repository metadata.
- All story, dialogue, item names, place names, spell names and mission text are written by the project author. Nothing is copied or translated from another game.
- Game rules (formulas, stats) may be inspired by general genre conventions but are tuned and named independently.
- Maps are original layouts. Recreating the idea of a well-known map is allowed only with new names and new art; no original map files are imported.
- Assets: only own work, CC0 or OFL-1.1 material, each with an entry in `assets.json`.
- Original-format files (`.res`, `.alm`, `.256`, `.smk`) never enter the repository; CI rejects them.
