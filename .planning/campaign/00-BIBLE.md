# THORNVAEL CAMPAIGN BIBLE (00-BIBLE)

## 1. World Vision & Tone

### 1.1 Aesthetic & Literary Register
Thornvael is a grounded, materialist dark-fantasy world where ancient cataclysms are experienced through logistics, ledgers, wet gunpowder, corroded bronze, and thermodynamic law. Magic is neither whimsical nor mystical: it is high-pressure telluric current governed by grounding rods, quartz wardstones, and meteoric iron valves. Necromancy is practiced as cold legal accounting under Third-Dynasty Probate Debt Law, where unpaid granary liens remain enforceable upon a debtor's skeleton after death.

### 1.2 Core Pillars of the Tone
- **Material Scarcity & Bureaucratic Dread**: Wars are fought over shaved silver groats from the burned Border Mint, casks of refined whale-oil, silted mountain cisterns, and salted barley.
- **Tragic, Rational Antagonists**: None of the four Act Bosses sees themselves as a cartoon villain. Each is an institutional commander or ancient warden driven to monstrous extremes by a collapsing continental system:
  - **Morvessa** bleeds the northern frontier dry with the Salt Tithe and unleashes ossuary broods to audit and enforce imperial arrears.
  - **Gorrathul** locks the mountain sluice-gates and starves the salt-parishes to hide the fact that Ironheart Bastion's legendary cisterns silted dry years ago.
  - **Vesperion** torches Oakhaven's fleet in a ruthless naval quarantine to stop mainland armies from crossing the straits and shattering the caldera seals.
  - **Ashkarath** guards a suffocating, ash-choked world order because unbinding the Primordial Ember turns mortal rulers into living fuel.
- **Cognitive Internal Voices**: Dialogues use internal veteran instincts (`[WAR LOGIC]`, `[TACTICAL CALCULUS]`, `[ASTRAL SHIVER]`, `[VETERAN FLESH]`, `[FRONTIER SKEPTICISM]`, `[QUARTERMASTER'S EYE]`, `[VISCERAL DREAD]`, `[PRIMORDIAL RECKONING]`, `[COHORT MEMORY]`) paired with tactile sensory beats *(cold tallow, sleet on iron, ozone and river silt)*.

## 2. Historical Timeline & The Overarching Threat

### 2.1 The Seven Anchor Seals & The Thermodynamic Crisis
Four centuries ago (**Year -412**), masons of the First Watch sank seven meteoric star-iron **Anchor Seals** into the southern volcanic caldera floor to bleed primordial magma pressure into the deep sea. In **Year -389**, the **Crown of Order** was forged from fallen star-iron and quenched in alpine snow, binding the sovereign's lineage to Thornvael's tectonic stability.

Over four hundred years, the Anchor Seals bled too much warmth from the upper provinces. Winters grew longer; northern harvests failed. In **Year -98**, dissident scholars expelled from the Capital Collegium published the *Treatise on Arcane Thermodynamics*, proving that invocations and wardstones function as pressure valves along continental leylines. In **Year -24**, **Arch-Mage Malrik** traded his eyesight to read subterranean magma veins, sparking the **Caldera Schism**: his Ember Court faction sought to crack the seventh Anchor Seal using focusing prisms to flood Thornvael with eternal summer—ignoring Ashkarath's warning that a ruptured caldera would incinerate the realm's memory and flesh.

### 2.2 The Cascade Across Four Provinces (Year 0)
1. **Map 1 — The Border Fords (`Act I`)**: Six years after burning Thornwick's Border Mint to cover up shaved silver coinage, **High Quartermaster Morvessa** enforces the brutal Salt Tithe. She pays **Chieftain Grok'tar's** gnoll warbands in stamped mint copper to slaughter the Sunken Ford watch and burn the toll ledgers, while breeding a parasitic silk-brood in the **Weeping Ossuary** beneath Ravenwatch Keep.
2. **Map 2 — The Redmarsh Wastes (`Act II`)**: Pursuing Morvessa's supply train south into the evaporated inland sea of Redmarsh, the Vanguard discovers that Ashen Coven blood-chanters have chiseled reverse wards into the twelve primeval **Quartz Wardstones**, unleashing static alkali storms. At the mountain throat, **Gorrathul** holds **Ironheart Bastion** behind twin causeways, hoarding a dry aquifer while chained prisoners in the **Deep Quarry** unearth flooded Third-Dynasty probate vaults.
3. **Map 3 — The Iron Coast (`Act III`)**: Descending the coastal switchbacks to the walled port of **Oakhaven**, the Vanguard finds the bay choked by **Lord Admiral Vesperion's** Black Fleet. Vesperion bombards the sea-walls, floods the **Sunken Citadel**, and sends fire-hulks into **Harborwatch** to destroy every sea-going hull before the Vanguard can embark for the southern volcanic archipelago.
4. **Map 4 — The Obsidian Caldera (`Act IV`)**: Crossing the ash-darkened straits in patched carracks, the Vanguard lands at **Ironcamp** and marches down the basalt highway through the **Gauntlet of Iron** and **Three-Roads Watch**. At the **Crossroads of Headless Kings**, the Commander chooses the fate of the realm before ascending the **Obsidian Dais** to face **First Warden Ashkarath**.

### 3.1 City 1: Thornwick Market (`town-thornwick`)
- **People & Culture**: Mud-stained frontier pragmatists—timber sawyers, peat-cutters, eel-weir trappers, and provincial bridge-pickets who value dry bowstrings, honest coin weight, and unbroken watch rotations over capital titles.
- **History**: Raised around the timber palisades protecting the Alder River crossings and the provincial Border Mint. Stripped of grain, brine, and iron ore by Morvessa's emergency Salt Tithe (**Year -9**), and scarred by the arson of the Border Mint (**Year -6**).
- **Strategic Importance**: Controls the northern timber mills (`Greywood`), the granite crossing (`Alder Bridge`), and the dwarven drainage tunnels (`Undervault Links`) leading south toward the Redmarsh depression.

### 3.2 City 2: Redmarsh Outpost (`town-redmarsh`)
- **People & Culture**: Alkali-crusted salt-caravaneers, cistern-guards, dune-outriders, and probate mortuary clerks. Bound by strict water-rationing oaths ("half a pint of boiled rainwater at sunrise, a gill of vinegar-brine at dusk"), they weigh water on apothecary scales and bind indebted corpses in copper wire to patrol subterranean salt-vaults.
- **History**: Built on limestone stilts and calcified leviathan ribcages inside an inland sea that evaporated after the **Year -184** fault-shift. Shielded from southern dust-squalls by twelve primeval quartz wardstones erected in **Year -312**.
- **Strategic Importance**: Guards the corduroy causeways, the black ironstone seams of **Deep Quarry**, and the twin mountain approaches to **Ironheart Bastion**, the sole pass descending to the Iron Coast.

### 3.3 City 3: Oakhaven Harbor (`town-oakhaven`)
- **People & Culture**: Shrewd maritime tariff-collectors, drydock shipwrights, bronze-culverin gunners, lighthouse keepers, and expelled thermodynamic scholars who calibrate rune-circles with copper wire and smoked quartz lenses.
- **History**: Chartered in **Year -156** under the Whale-Oil Charter where the Greywash Canal meets the storm-lashed sea. Its outer barbicans incorporate drowned Age-of-Chains fortresses and tidal canal locks.
- **Strategic Importance**: Possesses the only drydocks, whale-oil magazines, and sea-going carracks capable of ferrying an expeditionary force across the boiling volcanic straits to the southern archipelago.

### 3.4 City 4: Ironcamp Forge-Hold (`town-ironcamp`)
- **People & Culture**: Keloid-scarred siege-smiths wearing wet leather aprons and smoked-quartz goggles alongside sightless, iron-collared monks of the Scorched Penitents and spectral oath-keepers of the First Watch.
- **History**: Established over open geothermal floor-fissures at the foot of the basalt highway leading to the Infernal Citadel. Here meteoric star-iron is drawn at white heat and quenched within four heartbeats in sulfur-rich caldera brine.
- **Strategic Importance**: The final anvil and staging ground before the **Gauntlet of Iron**, **Three-Roads Watch**, and the **Obsidian Dais** where the seven Anchor Seals hold back the mountain's core.

## 6. Resolution Across the 4 Huge Maps

1. **Map 1 (`The Border Fords & Weeping Ossuary`)**: The Commander rallies Thornwick, holds Sunken Ford against Grok'tar, purges Morvessa's brood-nest inside the Weeping Ossuary beneath Ravenwatch, holds Alder Bridge for the refugee evacuation, and defeats Morvessa's siege host at Ashbrook Gatehouse—recovering the *Border Mint Assay Ledger* and *Morvessa's Brass Seal Ring*, which expose the southern bullion drain into Redmarsh.
2. **Map 2 (`The Redmarsh & Ironheart Cisterns`)**: Operating from Redmarsh Outpost, the Commander repairs the three ruptured Quartz Wardstones, liberates the chained ironstone miners in Deep Quarry, purges the Third-Dynasty probate vaults, and storms the twin causeways of Ironheart Bastion to slay Gorrathul—recovering the *Forked Sluice Key* and *Notched Meteoric Shield-Core*, opening the mountain descent to the Iron Coast.
3. **Map 3 (`The Iron Coast & Drowned Citadel`)**: From Oakhaven Harbor, the Commander seals the Bailey Truce, rescues the cut-off vanguard on Hourglass Spit, drains the tidal locks of the Sunken Citadel, reactivates the sea-cliff bronze culverins, and boards Vesperion's burning flagship at Harborwatch—recovering the *Salt-Corroded Astrolabe* locked due south toward the volcanic plume.
4. **Map 4 (`The Obsidian Caldera & Cinder Throne`)**: Staging from Ironcamp Forge-Hold, the Commander tempers star-iron gear in caldera fissures, intercepts Arch-Mage Malrik on the Ashveil stairs, breaches the Gauntlet of Iron and Three-Roads Watch, and reaches the **Crossroads of Headless Kings**:
   - **Path of the Star-Iron Compact (`Ending A — The Crown of Order`)**: Ground the focusing prisms and defeat Ashkarath to vent the caldera pressure into the abyss, restoring clean skies and constitutional order to Thornvael.
   - **Path of the Living Furnace (`Ending B — Ember Reckoning`)**: Shatter the last seal on the Obsidian Dais, slay Ashkarath, and fuse the Primordial Ember with the Commander's own flesh to rule Thornvael from an eternal throne of ash and steel.

## 7. Glossary of Terms

- **Anchor Seals (The Seven Valves)**: Seven colossal meteoric star-iron valves sunk into the southern caldera floor in Year -412 by First Watch masons to bleed primordial magma pressure into the deep ocean.
- **Arcane Thermodynamics**: The scientific doctrine codified by the expelled Thermodynamic Conclave in Year -98, demonstrating that spells and ley-wards obey conservation of pressure, capacitance, and copper-wire grounding rather than divine favor.
- **Bailey Truce**: The wartime compact signed at Oakhaven in Year 0 between the northern mercenary vanguard and the Oakhaven Maritime Guild, trading sea-gate defense for whale-oil reserves and transport carracks.
- **Caldera Schism**: The Year -24 split in the Crown College when Arch-Mage Malrik blinded himself to read magma veins and led half the realm's arcanists south to unbind the seventh Anchor Seal.
- **Crown of Order**: The star-iron circlet forged in Year -389 and quenched in alpine snow, linking the sovereign's life-force to the stability of the seven Anchor Seals.
- **Crossroads of Headless Kings (`The Threshold`)**: The obsidian plaza before the Infernal Citadel where three northern kings were beheaded in Year -129 for refusing the caldera maintenance tithe, and where the Commander chooses between Ending A and Ending B.
- **First Compact**: The Year -340 treaty signed in iron and tallow between dwarven smith-clans and northern border lords, establishing the Undervault drainage galleries and Alder Bridge maintenance oaths.
- **Gall-Ink & Tallow Writ**: Official probate and requisition parchment sealed with mutton fat and acidic oak-gall ink; used by the Lower-Parish Scriptorium to bind indebted corpses into posthumous guard service.
- **Probate Debt Law**: Third-Dynasty legal code (**Year -275**) decreeing that unpaid granary and tax arrears survive death; debtors' ribs are woven with copper wire and their viscera sealed in bitumen canopic jars until kin redeem their tally-tablets in weighed silver.
- **Quartz Wardstones**: Twelve primeval quartz monoliths raised across the Redmarsh plateau in Year -312 to anchor coastal rain-clouds and pin toxic alkali dust below the mountain passes.
- **Salt Tithe**: The emergency requisition decree drafted by High Quartermaster Morvessa in Year -9, stripping northern border parishes of brine, grain, and bog-iron ore while concealing clipped silver groats at the burned Border Mint.
- **Vitrified Slag**: Translucent green glass formed when bog-iron ore is smelted with crushed river-quartz and peat-charcoal at extreme draft; prized by mages as a high-capacitance conductor for static lightning.
- **Whale-Oil Charter**: The Year -156 maritime monopoly granted to Oakhaven's merchant council, funding the great prism lighthouse, harbor boom-chains, and bronze sea-wall culverins.
